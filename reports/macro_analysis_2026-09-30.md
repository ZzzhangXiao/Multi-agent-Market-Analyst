## Macro Interpretation

**1. Rate environment**  
- **Label:** **RATES – MIXED** (deterministic signal)  
- **Data:** Fed funds rate is **3.63%** and has been essentially flat over the past month and three months (‑0.01 % over six months).  

**Implication:** The policy stance is neither clearly tightening nor easing; the Fed appears to be holding rates steady while monitoring incoming data.

---

**2. Inflation dynamics**  
- **Label:** **INFLATION – FALLING** (deterministic signal)  
- **Data:** CPI‑YoY is **3.713 %** and, although the month‑over‑month change (+0.173 ppt) is positive, the six‑month change is **+1.048 ppt**, indicating a deceleration from earlier periods. The CPI index level is **334.131** and its trend is marked **rising**, but the *inflation rate* (cpi_yoy_pct) is trending downwards.  

**Implication:** Inflation pressure is easing, providing a tailwind for fixed‑income assets and reducing the upside risk for equities.

---

**3. Labor market**  
- **Label:** **LABOR – TIGHTENING** (deterministic signal)  
- **Data:** Unemployment is **4.1 %**, flat month‑to‑month, down **0.2 ppt** over three months and **0.3 ppt** over six months, with a flat trend.  

**Implication:** A tightening labor market supports consumer spending and underpins demand‑driven equities, though the modest pace suggests no imminent overheating.

---

**4. Yield environment**  
- **Label:** **YIELDS – ELEVATED** (deterministic signal)  
- **Data:** 10‑year Treasury yield is **4.68 %**, up **0.08 %** m/m, **0.20 %** q/q, and **0.55 %** over six months; the trend is **rising**.  

**Implication:** Yield levels are high relative to recent history, creating valuation pressure on growth‑oriented stocks and real‑estate assets that are sensitive to borrowing costs.

---

**5. Macro regime implications**  
- **Regime classification:** **Goldilocks (medium confidence)** – the regime is *qualified* rather than certain because confidence is only medium.  
- **Support scores:** Goldilocks = 2, Stagflation = 1, Reflation = 1, Deflationary bust = 0.  

**Interpretation:** The economy is viewed as broadly balanced—moderate growth, inflation easing, and labor tightening—yet the medium confidence flag warns that a shift toward either stagflation or reflation remains plausible.
